Spindle Hop

Tower 1/5: Place –Best on Normal: –

Rival skill

Everyone runs up the spiral on their own. Step between the three lanes with ← and → (or A and D): left is toward the tower, right is toward the edge. Jump pink hurdles, gaps and rolling drums with Space, W or ↑. Hold S, ↓ or Shift to duck under blue beams. Grey walls can only be dodged. Laser gates switch between low and high, so check their colour. On a touchscreen, use the pads, or swipe sideways, tap the top half to jump and hold the bottom half to duck. Every tower is built fresh each time.
