Spindle Hop

Zone 1: Distance 0 mBest on Normal: –

Difficulty
Difficulty

An endless run up a spiral tower. ← and → (or A and D) move between the three lanes, ↑, W or Space jumps, and holding ↓ or S ducks. Jump pink hurdles, gaps and rolling drums, duck blue beams, and dodge grey walls. Laser gates switch between low and high. Grab gems for points, and keep a clean streak going to raise your multiplier. On a touchscreen, swipe anywhere: left or right to change lane, up to jump, down to duck (hold to stay ducked). You can chain swipes without lifting your finger.
